📖 Case Study Overview

This case study documents practical traffic light repair and maintenance work carried out in Songea Town, Ruvuma Region.

The work focused on investigating an operational fault, performing appropriate inspections and tests, carrying out corrective maintenance, and verifying system operation after the repair.

The troubleshooting process followed a systematic approach rather than replacing components based on assumptions.

---

🔍 1. Initial Inspection

The maintenance process started with a visual and functional inspection of the traffic signal system.

The inspection focused on:

- Traffic signal heads
- Controller equipment
- Power supply
- Electrical connections
- Wiring
- Terminals and connectors
- Protection devices
- Visible physical damage
- Signs of overheating or water ingress

The purpose of the initial inspection was to identify possible fault areas before carrying out component-level diagnosis.

---

⚡ 2. Electrical & System Testing

Appropriate electrical and functional tests were performed to help isolate the source of the fault.

The diagnostic process considered:

- Power supply condition
- Electrical connections
- Controller operation
- Signal head operation
- Wiring and terminals
- System response

Measurements and observations were evaluated together with the overall circuit/system condition.

«A component was not considered faulty from a single abnormal reading alone. Additional checks were required to support the diagnosis.»

---

🔧 3. Fault Diagnosis

The fault diagnosis followed a systematic troubleshooting process:

Reported / Observed Fault
          ↓
Visual Inspection
          ↓
Power & Connection Checks
          ↓
System Testing
          ↓
Fault Isolation
          ↓
Corrective Action
          ↓
Retesting
          ↓
System Verification

This approach helped avoid unnecessary replacement of components before sufficient evidence was available.

---

🛠️ 4. Corrective Maintenance

After the fault area was identified through inspection and testing, corrective maintenance was performed.

Activities included, where applicable:

- Electrical connection correction
- Wiring/terminal inspection and correction
- Equipment adjustment
- Faulty connection rectification
- Component replacement where supported by testing
- Functional restoration

«Specific component-level details will be documented only where they are confirmed by field measurements and project records.»

---

🧪 5. Testing After Repair

After the corrective work, the traffic signal system was tested again.

Verification included:

- Power supply verification
- Signal head operation
- Controller functionality
- Wiring and connection checks
- Signal sequence verification
- System response
- Overall functional operation

The purpose of the retest was to confirm that the fault had been resolved and that the system was operating correctly after maintenance.

---

✅ 6. Final Result

Following the maintenance and repair activities, the system was functionally tested and verified.

Result: System restoration and functional verification completed. ✅

📚 Lessons Learned

This maintenance activity reinforced several important troubleshooting principles:

- Do not judge a component from one measurement alone.
- Check the complete circuit/system.
- Verify power and connections before replacing components.
- Use measurements and observations together.
- Isolate the fault before carrying out corrective action.
- Retest the system after repair.
- Document the repair process and final result.
